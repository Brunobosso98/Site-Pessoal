# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves two overlapping audiences: recruiters or technical leaders evaluating Bruno Martins for full-stack and automation roles, and business clients evaluating whether he can deliver practical systems, integrations, AI assistants, and process automation. Visitors are usually scanning quickly, comparing credibility, and looking for proof that the work can reduce manual effort, improve reliability, or create new digital workflows.

## Product Purpose

This is Bruno Martins' personal curriculum and portfolio site. Its job is to present him as a commercially minded full-stack developer and automation specialist, show credible project evidence, and guide visitors from initial trust to direct contact. Success means a visitor understands what Bruno builds, sees enough proof in the projects to trust the execution, and contacts him to discuss work, hiring, or a business opportunity.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, impactful. The voice should feel technically confident, commercially clear, and direct, with enough energy to stand apart from generic developer portfolios. The emotional goal is earned confidence: visitors should feel that Bruno can understand a business process, build the software, automate the repetitive parts, and explain the result without noise.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic SaaS landing page, a purple template portfolio, an AI-generated hero with vague claims, or a rigid traditional resume. Avoid decorative gradient text, glassmorphism as default, repeated icon-card grids, empty buzzwords, excessive "modern and innovative" copy, and layouts that feel interchangeable with any developer portfolio.

## Design Principles

Proof before claim: lead visitors from positioning into concrete projects, outcomes, screenshots, and implementation details.

Commercial clarity: frame technical skills around business value, time saved, risk reduced, and systems delivered.

Premium with edge: feel polished and current without falling into generic SaaS aesthetics or overproduced AI visuals.

Contact is the conversion: projects build confidence, but the page should ultimately make it natural to start a conversation.

Motion earns attention: animation should clarify hierarchy, guide the scroll, or make the portfolio feel built by a skilled frontend developer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences and ensure the experience remains complete without animation. The site should work well on mobile, desktop, and common browser zoom levels, with Portuguese copy that stays clear and readable.
